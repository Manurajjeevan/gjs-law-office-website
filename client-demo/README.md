# GJS Advocates & Solicitors — Website Demo

A nine-section, scroll-led law-firm website concept for GJS Advocates & Solicitors, Bengaluru. Its background is the handcrafted CSS 3D office with an eased, multi-angle camera path; it does not turn the office photos into slides. The updated palette pairs deep evergreen, warm ivory and aged brass, with copper accents.
